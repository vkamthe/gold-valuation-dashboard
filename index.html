📖 Valuation Framework, Method & Update Schedule ▼

💵 Gold / M2 Money Supply

Measures fiat currency creation backed by global gold stock (~212,500 tonnes). At the 1980 inflation peak, Gold market cap equaled 100%+ of US M2. At the 2000 trough, it dropped to ~15%.

🏛 Central Bank Reserve Buying

Tracks net annual gold purchases by global central banks (metric tonnes/year) and central bank gold as a % of total global foreign exchange reserves.

📊 Real Gold Price (CPI-Adjusted)

Strips out 50+ years of US CPI inflation to evaluate gold's true purchasing power in constant 2026 USD terms.

⚖️ Gold / Silver & Oil Ratios

Cross-asset purchasing power. Historical Gold/Silver ratio averages ~62.5, while Gold/Oil averages ~18 barrels/oz.
